PRIVACY FIRST

Your information is saved on this device

Entries are stored in this browser's local storage. There are no accounts or server uploads. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to see the entries; clearing browser data can erase them. Avoid shared devices and avoid entering sensitive clinical details.
